# Attendees register without Accounts

The auth template we started from paired "Register" and "Sign In" for the same person. Here an Attendee submits a Registration through a public form, with no Account and no password. A one-off visit to a two-day Event is not worth the friction of creating credentials. Accounts exist only for Staff signing in to the Back Office.

## Consequences

An Attendee cannot view, change or cancel their Registration after submitting it. Any of that has to go through Staff, or through a separate mechanism added later. Do not "fix" this by adding attendee sign-up without revisiting this decision.
